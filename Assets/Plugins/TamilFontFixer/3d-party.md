### TAC-Barathi (TACE16)

- **Author:** Government of Tamil Nadu

- **Description:** TACE16 is an alternate Tamil character encoding standard announced by the Government of Tamil Nadu.
- **License/Availability:** Provided free by Government of Tamil Nadu via Tamil Virtual Academy.
